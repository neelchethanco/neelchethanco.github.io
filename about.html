About Me

Pursuing a career in
propulsion & autonomous systems.

I'm an aerospace engineering student at the University of Maryland, focusing on propulsion and autonomous systems.

My long-term goal is to work on advanced propulsion, from electric thrusters to next-generation rocket engines, pushing the boundaries of how far we can go. I learn fastest by building: turning a CAD model into a welded chassis, or a set of equations into a working orbital simulator.

My work spans swarm robotics prototypes and MATLAB/Python simulations for multi-agent coordination, which has pulled me toward the intersection of controls, simulation, and AI applications in aerospace.
